About us

Who we are

Smart ADV Solutions. LLC is an advertising services company organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, USA, on July 10, 2023. We help brands, online stores and local businesses reach the right audience through targeted advertising, compelling creative and data-driven campaign management.

Our mission

To give businesses of every size access to professional, results-focused advertising with transparent reporting and clear pricing.

How we work

Every engagement starts with goals and budget. We then build the strategy, produce the creative, launch the campaigns and optimize them continuously.

Who we serve

E-commerce brands, retailers, service providers and startups in the United States and Europe looking to grow their customer base.

Services

What we do

End-to-end advertising services, delivered as individual projects or ongoing monthly management.

Digital Advertising

Paid campaigns on Google, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok and other platforms, with audience targeting and budget management.

Social Media Marketing

Content planning, account management and community growth to build brand awareness and engagement.

Creative Production

Ad copy, graphic design, banners and short-form video created to perform on each channel.

Media Planning & Buying

Selecting and purchasing advertising placements across online and offline media to maximize reach per dollar spent.

Analytics & Reporting

Conversion tracking, performance dashboards and regular reports so clients see exactly what their budget achieves.

Marketing Strategy

Market research, brand positioning and campaign planning tailored to each client’s goals and budget.
